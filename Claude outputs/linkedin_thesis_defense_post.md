Yesterday I successfully defended my thesis, "A Bayesian Approach to Digital Twins with Applications to Predictive Maintenance," completing my Advanced Master's in Statistical Data Analysis at Ghent University.

The thesis builds a Bayesian digital twin for predictive maintenance using particle filtering (Sequential Monte Carlo methods), applied to the NASA C-MAPSS turbofan engine dataset to track engine health and estimate remaining useful life.

I'm deeply grateful to my supervisors, Prof. Dr. Koen De Turck and Prof. Dr. Dieter Fiems, for their guidance, patience, and the care with which they read every draft.

This closes one chapter, and I'm excited for what comes next, building on the modeling and research skills this thesis gave me.
